Selected Publications
Two Heads Are Better Than One: Fake News Video Detection by Correlating with Neighbors
Peng Qi, Yuyang Zhao, Yufeng Shen, Wei Ji, Juan Cao, Tat-Seng Chua
ACL Findings, 2023

This paper proposes a model-agnostic cross-sample framework NEED for detecting fake news videos, which utilizes the neighbour relationship between the related news videos in a same event and thus can largely improve the performance of existing single-sample detectors. This paper is also a successful attempt to combine automatic fake news detection and fact-checking, and firstly proves the effectiveness of debunking videos in detection.

FakeSV: A Multimodal Benchmark with Rich Social Context for Fake News Detection on Short Video Platforms
Peng Qi, Yuyan Bu, Juan Cao, Wei Ji, Ruihao Shui, Junbin Xiao, Danding Wang, Tat-Seng Chua
AAAI, 2023

PDF / Code
We construct the largest Chinese fake news short video dataset FakeSV, which includes news content, user comments, and publisher profiles simultaneously. It contains 1,827 fake news videos, 1,827 real news videos and 1,884 debunking videos under 738 events. We provide explorary and insightful data analysis from the perspective of news content, social context and propagation. Moreover, we design a new multimodal detection model SV-FEND as the SOTA method in this benchmark. We also re-implement 11 single-modal and 4 multimodal methods as baseline methods. We have release the dataset and codes to facilitate this task.
